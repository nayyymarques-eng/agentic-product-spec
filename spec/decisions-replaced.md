# Replaced decisions

A decision that is no longer true moves here, from `decisions.md`, with the decision that replaces it. It keeps its ID, so a search still finds what it was and why it changed. Nothing in the spec may cite it: the spec check fails on a rule, a question or a decision that still does, and names it, so every place that followed the old decision is looked at again. What we learned says why it stopped being true and, when the decision had an Expected, where that expectation was wrong, so nobody, person or agent, brings it back without something new.
