# Decisions

The product decisions: choices the team made on purpose, so nobody flags them as errors and nobody has to ask again. A decision can come from user research, a market insight, the business strategy or a technical limit. None may go against an external rule in `sources/`.

Decisions live at two levels. This is the product decision log: decisions about the whole product, made for no single feature. "Applies to" says `product`, or names the flows and screens it touches. Every decision made for a feature lives in that feature's decision log, in its flow file under `## Decisions`, even when other features follow it too. When a decision is no longer true, it moves to `decisions-replaced.md`, with the ID of the one that replaces it. A decision someone proposes is `proposed` until the person in Decided by approves it and sets it to `decided`. Expected, when a decision has it, says what should change for the people who use the product if the decision is right, written before anyone knows, so the decision can be checked against it later. Considered instead, when a decision has it, names what was weighed and dropped, and why, so nobody proposes it again without something new.
